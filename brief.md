## AI

## Mobile
Mobile security protects smartphones and applications from malware, unauthorized access, and data theft. Strong passwords, software updates, and trusted applications help improve mobile security.
## Networking
Networking connects devices and systems so they can communicate and share information. Important concepts include IP addresses, DNS, routers, ports, and network protocols.
## Cybersecurity
Cybersecurity protects digital systems against threats such as phishing, malware, and unauthorized access. Common security practices include multi-factor authentication, regular updates, and secure passwords.
## Data
Data security protects information from unauthorized access, modification, disclosure, or loss. Encryption, access controls, and backups help protect data.
